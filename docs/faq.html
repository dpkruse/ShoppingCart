ShoppingCart Qualtrics Survey Instrument

The health score is the percentage of selected items in the cart that are tagged "healthy", rounded to the nearest whole number (0–100).

health_score = round( healthyCount / total × 100 )

Here is how it works step by step:

  • Every selected item across all 10 categories is checked against its tag field in the item registry — either "healthy", "unhealthy", or neutral.
  • healthyCount is the number of selected items tagged "healthy".
  • total is the total number of items selected (maximum 30).
  • If the cart is empty (total = 0), the score is 0 to avoid a divide-by-zero error.

Example: A cart of 30 items with 15 healthy, 10 neutral, and 5 unhealthy scores 50%.

The score is recalculated live on every checkbox change and written to the health_score Qualtrics embedded data field. It is visible in the sidebar only when debug mode is active (age = 99).

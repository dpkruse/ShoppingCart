Welcome — and thank you for taking part!

This study explores how people make decisions when shopping for groceries online. You'll be browsing a virtual supermarket and building a shopping cart — just like you would at home.

✅  Am I eligible to take part?

Please check that all of the following apply to you before continuing:

  • ✔ I identify as female
  • ✔ I like most foods and eat a wide variety
  • ✔ I have no food allergies or intolerances
  • ✔ I am not vegetarian, vegan, or pescatarian

If any of the above do not apply, please do not continue — this study may not be suitable for you.

📋  Before you begin

💻 Complete the study on a laptop or desktop computer — not a phone or tablet
🔇 Find a quiet room where you won't be interrupted
📵 Put your mobile phone on silent or turn it off
